How the real test works

  • During your naturalization interview, the USCIS officer asks up to 20 questions from the list of 128. You must answer at least 12 correctly to pass.
  • 65/20 special consideration: if you are 65 or older and have been a lawful permanent resident for 20+ years, you study only the 20 asterisked questions. The officer asks 10 of them, and 6 correct passes.
  • On the real test you answer out loud, but here you always get written options: when a question accepts more than one answer, every accepted answer appears as an option and any one of them counts as correct — in every mode, flashcards included.
  • Four questions — your U.S. senators, your U.S. representative, your governor, and your state capital — depend on where you live. This app fills them in with your Athens, Ohio answers so you can play them in every mode — update them anytime under My local answers.
  • Every question and answer can be read aloud — tap the speaker button on any card. On the real test the officer asks out loud, and the Listening Test mode practices exactly that.
